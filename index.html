Easy EMI – Privacy Policy

App: Easy EMI – Loan & Finance Calculator (Android, package com.easyemi.loancalculator)
Developer: Chetan Visavadiya · Effective date: 3 November 2026

In short

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your calculations, saved history and Money Manager transactions are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Premium subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it works the way you set it up:

  • Calculations you choose to save (the values you entered and the calculator used)
  • Income and expense entries you add in Money Manager, including amounts, categories, dates and notes
  • Your settings: language, currency, decimal places and theme
  • The most recent exchange rates, so the currency converter works offline

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete individual items in the app, clear your history in Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app's data in your device backup; that backup is managed by Google under your account.

2. Information collected by third-party services

Google's use of data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Ad privacy: where a consent form is required, you can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • No ads: a Premium subscription turns off all ads. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Delete your data: delete entries or clear history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data stored on your phone.

4. Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, which it uses to download exchange rates, load ads and connect to Google Play for subscriptions. The ads SDK also declares the advertising ID permission. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or SMS.

5. Children

Easy EMI is a finance tool meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security and sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we don't receive it in the first place. Connections to the services above use encrypted HTTPS. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

7. Financial disclaimer

All results in the app are estimates for planning purposes and are not financial advice. Banks and lenders may use different rounding, fees or compounding rules.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
